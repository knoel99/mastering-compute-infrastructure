# GPU CAPACITY AGREEMENT — **FICTIONAL EXAMPLE, WRITTEN FOR A COURSE. NOT A REAL CONTRACT, NOT A REAL PROVIDER.**

Between "Fictional Compute Provider" (the "Provider") and "the Customer".
Every number below is invented to exercise a review grid. Clause headings are stable identifiers used by the lab.

## 1. Term and commitment
1.1 Minimum Term: 36 months from the Start Date.
1.2 Committed Capacity: 512 GPUs. The Customer pays for the Committed Capacity every hour, whether used or not (take-or-pay).
1.3 Renewal: the agreement renews automatically for 12 months unless the Customer gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the term.
1.4 The Customer may not terminate for convenience.

## 2. Delivery
2.1 The estimated delivery date of the Committed Capacity is 1 March. Delivery may be made in tranches; the first tranche is 25% of the Committed Capacity and the remainder is delivered as available.
2.2 No remedy applies to a delayed delivery. The Customer is invoiced from the Start Date regardless of the delivered capacity.

## 3. Availability
3.1 Availability Target: 99.5% per calendar month, measured per cluster.
3.2 A node is counted as available if its management interface answers network probes. GPU errors, interconnect degradation, and storage unavailability are not Unavailability.
3.3 Scheduled maintenance of up to 24 hours per month, announced 48 hours in advance, is excluded. Emergency maintenance is excluded and needs no notice.
3.4 Service Credits: 5% of the monthly fee if availability is below 99.5% and at least 99.0%; 10% of the monthly fee if below 99.0%. The credit is capped at 10% of the monthly fee.
3.5 Service Credits are the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for unavailability. The Customer must claim within 30 days of the end of the month.

## 4. Replacement
4.1 The Provider will replace a failed node using commercially reasonable efforts and within a reasonable time. No replacement time is guaranteed.
4.2 A replacement may be of a different configuration or GPU generation that the Provider reasonably considers of equivalent or similar performance.
4.3 There is no acceptance test: capacity is deemed accepted on delivery.

## 5. Performance
5.1 The Provider gives no commitment on interconnect bandwidth, latency, storage throughput, or GPU clock frequency.

## 6. Support
6.1 Support is available on business days, 09:00 to 17:00 CET, by ticket. The target first response for a critical incident is 8 business hours.
6.2 There is no named technical contact and no escalation path.

## 7. Price
7.1 The unit price is fixed for the first 12 months. From the second year the Provider may increase prices once a year by up to 8%.
7.2 Power or cooling surcharges may be added "to reflect cost increases" with 30 days notice.

## 8. Data and exit
8.1 Data transfer out of the Provider's network is charged at the standard rate, including during and after termination.
8.2 Customer data is deleted within 30 days after termination. The Provider gives no deletion certificate.
8.3 The Provider may move the Committed Capacity to another data center with 30 days notice.

## 9. Liability and force majeure
9.1 The Provider's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the 3 months preceding the claim. The Provider is not liable for loss of data, profits, or training progress.
9.2 Force majeure includes shortages of components, supply-chain disruption, and unavailability of power.
9.3 The Provider may suspend service on reasonable suspicion of breach, without notice.
9.4 The Provider may terminate for convenience with 30 days notice, with no refund of prepaid fees.
