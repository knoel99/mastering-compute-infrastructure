1.1. Ce qu'on retient des États-Unis

  • Presque aucun master « HPC » en tant que tel. L'Illinois n'a pas de master HPC autonome ; Georgia Tech propose un MS en science computationnelle où le calcul haute performance est l'une des options de base ; le master en ligne OMSCS n'a pas de spécialisation HPC mais permet d'en composer une à partir de cours.
  • Le HPC est dans les cours. Les cours de référence sont publics et réutilisables : Stanford CS149 (calcul parallèle) et CS336, CMU 15-418, Deep Learning Systems, Machine Learning Systems et Berkeley CS267 (voir la page Ressources).
  • Des centres qui forment. Les laboratoires du département de l'Énergie (NERSC et d'autres) publient des formations d'accueil pour les utilisateurs ; le Student Cluster Competition de la conférence SC est l'équivalent américain des compétitions chinoises.
  • Limite de la lecture. Six programmes seulement ont été étudiés, la couverture de plusieurs thèmes d'exploitation y est inconnue, et le calcul d'une proportion « américaine » n'aurait aucun sens : lisez les fiches une à une.

2. Les programmes

2.1. MS in Computational Science and Engineering (CSE)

Confiance élevéeScience computationnelleUS

Établissement Georgia Institute of Technology (College of Computing / College of Engineering / College of Sciences; School of Computational Science and Engineering)
Diplôme MS
Durée Not stated on the catalogue or CSE pages read (length is expressed as credit hours; typical full-time duration not confirmed)
Crédits 30 US credit hours (12 core + 6 minor-related + 6 non-CS/CSE + 6 course or thesis, per 2026-2027 catalogue)
Langue English
Modalité on-site
Admission Bachelor's degree in a technical field (CS, mathematics, science or engineering); desired: calculus through multivariable, linear algebra, probability/statistics, 1-2 computing courses and proficiency in a high-level language (C/C++, Python, Java). Missing background may require extra coursework.
Machines citées PACE (Partnership for an Advanced Computing Environment): Phoenix cluster and ICE instructional cluster, listed with classroom/teaching resources; use by MS CSE courses not confirmed on the pages read

Structure. Master interdisciplinaire proposé conjointement par l'informatique, l'ingénierie et les sciences. Les étudiants choisissent quatre cours de base sur cinq (algèbre linéaire numérique, algorithmes pour la CSE, modélisation et simulation, analyse de données computationnelle, calcul haute performance), puis une mineure de spécialisation avec un domaine d'application, et terminent soit par 6 crédits de cours, soit par un mémoire soutenu. Le HPC est une option de base, pas une obligation.

Cours du tronc commun (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

  • CSE/MATH 6643 Numerical Linear Algebra (Méthodes numériques)
  • CSE 6140 Computational Science and Engineering Algorithms (Algorithmique parallèle, Modèles de performance, profilage)
  • CSE 6730 Modeling and Simulation: Foundations and Implementation (Simulation et applications)
  • CSE/ISYE 6740 Computational Data Analysis: Learning, Mining, and Computation (Apprentissage automatique et profond)
  • CSE 6220 High Performance Computing (Algorithmique parallèle, Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Passage de messages (MPI), Architecture des ordinateurs, Réseaux et interconnexions, Efficacité énergétique, Modèles de performance, profilage)

Mémoire ou stage. Either 6 credit hours of approved coursework (course option) or an MS thesis defended before a thesis committee (thesis option).

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. CSE page lists target careers in industry, national laboratories and government contractors (modelling and simulation, HPC, data mining).

Limites de la fiche. HPC is a core option among five; the CSE MS is breadth-oriented computational science, not an infrastructure/ops programme. Duration and the exact course schedule are not stated on the pages read. topic_coverage 1 for catalogue-only elective mentions. GPU programming, scheduling, sysadmin, storage and containers are null (no accessible syllabus examined), not 0.

2.2. Online Master of Science in Computer Science (OMSCS), with HPC/systems electives (CSE 6220, CS 7295, CS 6290, CS 7210)

Confiance élevéeMixteUS

Établissement Georgia Institute of Technology (College of Computing)
Diplôme MS
Durée Not stated on the degree-requirements page read (part-time online, course by course)
Crédits 30 US credit hours (10 courses)
Langue English
Modalité online
Admission Not read in detail. CSE 6220 suggests prior algorithms course (CS 6515 or undergraduate), C/C++ programming and *nix command-line experience.

Structure. Master d'informatique en ligne exigeant une spécialisation (15 à 18 heures de crédit) plus des options libres parmi tous les cours de l'OMSCS. Il n'existe pas de spécialisation HPC, mais le catalogue permet de composer un parcours HPC/accélérateurs : CSE 6220 (algorithmes parallèles, OpenMP/MPI), CS 7295 (matériel GPU et CUDA), CS 6290 (architecture des ordinateurs), CS 7210 (calcul distribué) et CS 6211 (conception de systèmes cloud).

Mémoire ou stage. None required by the degree-requirements page read.

Limites de la fiche. The degree is a general CS master delivered online; the HPC content is a set of electives, not a track. Course availability varies by term (Current Courses page). Cost, admission criteria and typical duration were not recorded. Topics not examined are null.

2.3. MS in Computer Science / MCS (Architecture, Compilers, Parallel Computing area) with the CSE Graduate Concentration

Confiance élevéeScience computationnelleUS

Établissement University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (Siebel School of Computing and Data Science; Computational Science and Engineering programme; NCSA)
Diplôme MS
Durée Not stated on the pages read (credit-hour based)
Crédits MS: 28 credit hours coursework + 4 credit hours thesis; MCS: 32 credit hours coursework only; CSE concentration: 16 graduate credit hours counted inside the degree
Langue English
Modalité on-site
Admission Not read in detail; the MCS page states no GRE is required for admission. The CSE concentration is open only to students already enrolled in participating graduate programmes.
Machines citées NCSA Delta (NSF-funded GPU system), listed on the NCSA site with an allocation process; use by coursework or master's students not confirmed on pages read

Structure. L'Illinois n'a pas de master HPC autonome. La voie HPC passe par le MS d'informatique (recherche, mémoire) ou le MCS (cours) dont le domaine de largeur « Architecture, compilateurs, calcul parallèle » contient CS 483/484 (programmation parallèle) et CS 533 (architecture parallèle), plus une concentration CSE facultative inscrite au relevé de notes (deux cours de base et deux options de calcul, 4 heures chacun) qui ajoute les algorithmes numériques parallèles (CS 554), la programmation parallèle et la programmation parallèle appliquée.

Cours du tronc commun (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

  • CS 484 Parallel Programming (Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Passage de messages (MPI), Modèles de performance, profilage, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • ECE 408 / CS 483 / CSE 408 Applied Parallel Programming (Programmation GPU, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • CS 420 / CSE 402 / ECE 492 Parallel Programming: Science and Engineering (Modèles de performance, profilage, Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Passage de messages (MPI))
  • CS 554 / CSE 512 Parallel Numerical Algorithms (Méthodes numériques, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • CSE 527 / CS 519 Scientific Visualization (CSE concentration core option) (Visualisation)

Mémoire ou stage. MS: 4 credit hours of thesis. MCS: coursework only. CSE concentration: thesis with a significant computational component, or (non-thesis MS) a 4-hour independent-study capstone project supervised by CSE-affiliated faculty.

Limites de la fiche. This record describes a pathway, not a branded HPC master: availability of each course per term and the actual student access to Delta were not verified. ECE 408 catalogue text does not name CUDA, hence gpu-prog = 1. Duration, admission requirements and cost not recorded. Distributed systems, scheduling, storage, containers, cloud and resilience are null (not examined).

2.4. MS in Computational Science, Engineering, and Mathematics (CSEM)

Confiance moyenneScience computationnelleUS

Établissement The University of Texas at Austin (Oden Institute for Computational Engineering and Sciences; TACC)
Diplôme MS
Durée Not stated on any page that could be fetched (Oden Institute pages fail TLS verification in this environment)
Crédits Thesis option 30, report option 33, no-thesis/no-report option 36 US credit hours; 24 hours must come from the three CSEM concentration areas (search-result snippet of the Oden master's page, not verified on the page itself)
Langue English
Modalité on-site
Admission Bachelor's in engineering, natural sciences, mathematics/statistics, computer science or a field using advanced mathematical modelling (search-result snippet, not verified). The same snippet says the programme is primarily PhD-track with few funded master's places.
Machines citées Oden Institute clusters (about 10 distributed-memory clusters, 64 to 1344 cores each); TACC supercomputers: Stampede3, Lonestar6, Vista, Frontera; Horizon (NSF LCCF, Phase 1 production Fall 2026)

Structure. Programme de master interdisciplinaire en trois domaines : mathématiques appliquées, analyse numérique et calcul scientifique, modélisation mathématique et applications. Les options du catalogue comprennent le calcul parallèle et l'architecture des ordinateurs. Les étudiants utilisent les clusters de l'Oden et les supercalculateurs du TACC. Le cours CSE 380 donne une introduction pratique au matériel et au génie logiciel du HPC ; CSE 397H est un stage de recherche de master.

Cours du tronc commun (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

  • CSE 383C Numerical Analysis: Linear Algebra (Méthodes numériques)
  • CSE 380 Tools and Techniques of Computational Science (Architecture des ordinateurs, Modèles de performance, profilage, Génie logiciel HPC)
  • CSE 389C Introduction to Mathematical Modeling in Science and Engineering I (Simulation et applications)

Mémoire ou stage. Thesis (CSE 698), master's report (CSE 398R) or coursework-only option; optional graduate research internship course CSE 397H.

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. CSE 397H internship in a research/industrial setting. TACC Frontera Fellowships for graduate students.

Limites de la fiche. The Oden Institute site (oden.utexas.edu, www.ices.utexas.edu) fails TLS verification (incomplete certificate chain) from this environment, so the programme page, handbook PDF and degree requirements could not be read. Course evidence comes from the archived 2025-26 UT catalogue; the current 2026-27 catalogue page for the programme exposes only the facilities text. Which courses are required vs optional could not be confirmed, so the core/elective split is indicative (catalogue courses in the CSE prefix). MPI, GPU, scheduling, storage and similar topics are null, not 0. Confidence capped at medium: part of the evidence comes from memory.

2.5. MS in Computational and Mathematical Engineering (general track plus Data Science, Imaging Science and Mathematical & Computational Finance tracks)

Confiance élevéeScience computationnelleUS

Établissement Stanford University (Institute for Computational and Mathematical Engineering, ICME)
Diplôme MS
Durée Not stated as a number of years on the pages read; admission for autumn quarter only; part-time/remote study possible via CGOE but on-campus attendance required
Crédits 45 units of coursework (courses numbered 200 or above, at least 36 graded units), no thesis required
Langue English
Modalité on-site
Admission Recommended: strong mathematics (linear algebra, numerical methods, probability, stochastics) and programming proficiency in C and MATLAB (Python/R depending on track). No specific background requirement stated by the bulletin.
Machines citées ICME clusters (CPU nodes with Infiniband and GPU nodes), used by ICME students with restricted partitions for certain courses

Structure. Master centré sur les mathématiques appliquées : cours fondamentaux (12 unités), calcul et programmation (6 unités), options de largeur (15), options spécialisées (9) et séminaires (3) dans la voie générale. Le bulletin cite une formation au calcul matriciel, à l'optimisation, à la résolution d'EDP, aux algorithmes d'ordinateurs parallèles et aux nouveaux paradigmes de calcul. L'ICME exploite ses propres clusters avec nœuds CPU et GPU et partitions de cours restreintes. Le contenu HPC et systèmes pour le ML est accessible par des options de l'informatique de Stanford (par exemple CS 149, CS 336), dont l'éligibilité comme options de l'ICME n'a pas été confirmée.

Mémoire ou stage. No thesis required. Optional 'Master of Science with Distinction in Research': at least three quarters of research ending in a written thesis (CME 291).

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. ICME publishes an employment report for MS graduates; Affiliate Program runs professional development workshops (e.g. LLM agents workshop).

Limites de la fiche. The ICME MS is not an HPC master; it is computational mathematics with a programming requirement. ExploreCourses (JS only) and the bulletin's course tables were not readable, so required course codes (e.g. CME 213 'parallel computing using MPI, OpenMP and CUDA', known from memory only) are not listed. The CS 149 and CS 336 records are indicative of the Stanford CS offer, they are not confirmed ICME requirements. Duration in years not recorded.

Diplôme SM
Durée 12-18 months for self-supported or fellowship students; allow two years if funded by RA/TA positions
Crédits 108 MIT units total (core 36 + restricted electives 24 + unrestricted elective 12 + thesis 36)
Langue English
Modalité on-site

Trois matières de base choisies parmi quatre (modélisation et simulation, méthodes d'optimisation, méthodes numériques pour les EDP, introduction aux méthodes numériques), 24 unités d'options restreintes dans une large liste interdépartementale (dont 18.337[J] Calcul parallèle et apprentissage scientifique, apprentissage automatique, mécanique des fluides, optimisation), une option libre de niveau master et un mémoire de 36 unités.

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. CSE programme site has an Industry Engagement section (not read in detail).

3. Autour des diplômes : centres, écoles, compétitions

  • Stanford CS149 Parallel Computing (public syllabus, assignments, Fall 2025) (cours-ouvert) : Best public syllabus found for the hardware/software boundary of parallel systems: multicore and SIMD, work scheduling, CUDA, DNN execution on GPUs, datacenter-scale AI serving, coherence and synchronisation, with programming assignments including CUDA kernels and a Trainium2 kernel.
  • Stanford CS336 Language Modeling from Scratch (Spring 2026 materials, assignments, recordings) (cours-ouvert) : A public course where one assignment is explicitly systems: profile, write a Triton FlashAttention2 kernel and build a memory-efficient distributed training loop, which is the user-level view of what a GPU-cluster platform must serve.
  • CMU 15-418/15-618 Parallel Computer Architecture and Programming (Fall 2026 site) (cours-ouvert) : Public syllabus and assignments for parallel hardware and programming (multicore, GPUs, 'C-like' parallel languages); sister course of Stanford CS149 with a systems-programming flavour.
  • Deep Learning Systems (dlsyscourse.org, CMU; build the 'Needle' framework with CPU/CUDA backends) (cours-ouvert) : Hands-on view of what sits under PyTorch: automatic differentiation, an NDArray library with CPU/CUDA backends, memory coalescing and tiling; useful background before operating GPU training platforms.
  • CMU 15-442/15-642 Machine Learning Systems (public schedule, Spring 2026) (cours-ouvert) : Public schedule covering data/ZeRO, model and pipeline parallelism, GPU architecture and CUDA, GEMM on modern GPUs and LLM serving: the most direct ML-systems syllabus found for a training/inference platform engineer.
  • UC Berkeley CS267 Applications of Parallel Computers (Spring 2025 site) (cours-ouvert) : Long-running reference course (Demmel, Yelick and others) on programming parallel computers for science and engineering; public site with syllabus; part of the Berkeley Designated Emphasis in Computational Science and Engineering.
  • SC Student Cluster Competition (SCC) at the SC conference (compétition) : Teams of students build, tune and run HPC applications on a cluster in a competition setting; the best hands-on route to cluster operation skills for a student, and a recruiting ground for HPC centres.
  • DOE facility user training (NERSC New User Training and SpinUp workshops; OLCF training calendar and archives) (formation-courte) : National computing centres publish training for their users; NERSC lists new-user training and SpinUp workshops and OLCF archives materials for Frontier. Details of content were not readable (JavaScript calendar), so use as pointers to current events.
