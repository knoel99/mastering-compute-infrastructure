### MS in Computational Science and Engineering (CSE) {#us-gatech-ms-cse}

| Établissement | Georgia Institute of Technology (College of Computing / College of Engineering / College of Sciences; School of Computational Science and Engineering) |
| Diplôme | MS |
| Durée | Not stated on the catalogue or CSE pages read (length is expressed as credit hours; typical full-time duration not confirmed) |
| Crédits | 30 US credit hours (12 core + 6 minor-related + 6 non-CS/CSE + 6 course or thesis, per 2026-2027 catalogue) |
| Langue | English |
| Modalité | on-site |
| Admission | Bachelor's degree in a technical field (CS, mathematics, science or engineering); desired: calculus through multivariable, linear algebra, probability/statistics, 1-2 computing courses and proficiency in a high-level language (C/C++, Python, Java). | Machines citées | PACE (Partnership for an Advanced Computing Environment): Phoenix cluster and ICE instructional cluster, listed with classroom/teaching resources; use by MS CSE courses not confirmed on the pages read |

**Structure.** Interdisciplinary MS jointly offered by Computing, Engineering and Sciences. Students pick four of five core courses (numerical linear algebra, CSE algorithms, modelling and simulation, computational data analysis, High Performance Computing), then a specialisation minor with an application area, and finish with either 6 credits of coursework or a defended thesis. HPC is one core option, not mandatory.

**Cours du tronc commun** (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

-   CSE/MATH 6643 Numerical Linear Algebra (Méthodes numériques)
-   CSE 6140 Computational Science and Engineering Algorithms (Algorithmique parallèle, Modèles de performance, profilage)
-   CSE 6730 Modeling and Simulation: Foundations and Implementation (Simulation et applications)
-   CSE/ISYE 6740 Computational Data Analysis: Learning, Mining, and Computation (Apprentissage automatique et profond)
-   CSE 6220 High Performance Computing (Algorithmique parallèle, Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Passage de messages (MPI), Architecture des ordinateurs, Réseaux et interconnexions, Efficacité énergétique, Modèles de performance, profilage)

**Mémoire ou stage.** Either 6 credit hours of approved coursework (course option) or an MS thesis defended before a thesis committee (thesis option).

**Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche.** CSE page lists target careers in industry, national laboratories and government contractors (modelling and simulation, HPC, data mining).

**Limites de la fiche.** HPC is a core option among five; the CSE MS is breadth-oriented computational science, not an infrastructure/ops programme. Duration and the exact course schedule are not stated on the pages read. topic_coverage 1 for catalogue-only elective mentions. GPU programming, scheduling, sysadmin, storage and containers are null (no accessible syllabus examined), not 0.

### Online Master of Science in Computer Science (OMSCS), with HPC/systems electives (CSE 6220, CS 7295, CS 6290, CS 7210) {#us-gatech-omscs}

**Structure.** Online CS master requiring one specialisation (15-18 credit hours) plus free electives from any OMSCS course. There is no HPC specialisation, but the catalogue lets a student assemble an HPC/accelerator track: CSE 6220 (parallel algorithms, OpenMP/MPI), CS 7295 (GPU hardware and CUDA), CS 6290 (computer architecture), CS 7210 (distributed computing) and CS 6211 (cloud system design).

**Mémoire ou stage.** None required by the degree-requirements page read.

**Limites de la fiche.** The degree is a general CS master delivered online; the HPC content is a set of electives, not a track. Course availability varies by term (Current Courses page). Cost, admission criteria and typical duration were not recorded. Topics not examined are null.

### MS in Computer Science / MCS (Architecture, Compilers, Parallel Computing area) with the CSE Graduate Concentration {#us-uiuc-ms-cs-cse}

| Langue | English |

**Structure.** Illinois has no stand-alone HPC master. The HPC route is a CS MS (research, thesis) or MCS (coursework) whose breadth area 'Architecture, Compilers, Parallel Computing' contains CS 483/484 (parallel programming), CS 533 (parallel architecture), plus an optional transcript-listed CSE concentration (two core + two computing electives, 4 hours each) that adds parallel numerical algorithms (CS 554), parallel programming and applied parallel programming.

**Cours du tronc commun** (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

-   CS 484 Parallel Programming (Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Passage de messages (MPI), Modèles de performance, profilage, Algorithmique parallèle)
-   ECE 408 / CS 483 / CSE 408 Applied Parallel Programming (Programmation GPU, Algorithmique parallèle)
-   CS 420 / CSE 402 / ECE 492 Parallel Programming: Science and Engineering (Modèles de performance, profilage, Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Passage de messages (MPI))
-   CS 554 / CSE 512 Parallel Numerical Algorithms (Méthodes numériques, Algorithmique parallèle)
-   CSE 527 / CS 519 Scientific Visualization (CSE concentration core option) (Visualisation)

**Mémoire ou stage.** MS: 4 credit hours of thesis. MCS: coursework only. CSE concentration: thesis with a significant computational component, or (non-thesis MS) a 4-hour independent-study capstone project supervised by CSE-affiliated faculty.

**Limites de la fiche.** This record describes a pathway, not a branded HPC master: availability of each course per term and the actual student access to Delta were not verified. ECE 408 catalogue text does not name CUDA, hence gpu-prog = 1. Duration, admission requirements and cost not recorded. Distributed systems, scheduling, storage, containers, cloud and resilience are null (not examined).
