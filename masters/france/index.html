Master Calcul haute performance, simulation (CHPS) - M1, M2 IHPS and M2 MSCHP

Confiance élevéeHPC (cœur)FR

Établissement Université de Versailles Saint-Quentin (UVSQ, ISTY) / Université Paris-Saclay, with ENS Paris-Saclay and INSTN/CEA
Diplôme M2 (preceded by M1 CHPS)
Durée 4 semesters over 2 years (M1 + M2); the M2 alone is 2 semesters (1 academic semester + 1 internship semester); Bac+4 entry level for M2
Crédits M2: 60 ECTS (S3: 30 ECTS and 350 h of teaching; S4: 30 ECTS internship per UVSQ page, while the Paris-Saclay catalogue lists the internship unit as 24 ECTS). M1 total not stated on the pages read.
Langue French (technical English units; programme page says language of teaching: Français)
Modalité on-site
Admission M1: bachelor-level degree equivalent in computer science, mathematics, physics or mechanics (35 places). M2: M1 CHPS (automatic admission for UVSQ M1 CHPS), or a scientific M1 / engineering school with parallel computing (architecture, algorithms, programming) and applied mathematics; selection on file otherwise.

Structure. Part of the national network of CHPS masters. The M1 mixes mathematics (numerical analysis, modelling) with HPC (parallel architectures, parallel programming, distributed algorithms, software engineering for HPC and AI). The M2 has a common core (multicore programming, hardware accelerators, performance evaluation, numerical programming, data/learning, continuum mechanics) followed by one of two tracks: IHPS (high-performance computing, compilers, code optimisation, quantum computing) or MSCHP (modelling and simulation, PDE discretisation, domain decomposition, model reduction). Strongly project-oriented, ending with a 5-6 month internship.

Cours du tronc commun (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

  • M1 - Parallel Architectures (Architecture Parallèle) (Architecture des ordinateurs, Modèles de performance, profilage)
  • M1 - Algorithms and Parallel Programming (Algorithmique et Programmation Parallèle) (Algorithmique parallèle, Passage de messages (MPI), Mémoire partagée (OpenMP))
  • M1 - Distributed Algorithms (Algorithmique Distribuée) (Systèmes distribués, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • M1 - Software Engineering for HPC & AI (Génie logiciel pour le calcul HPC/IA) (Génie logiciel HPC)
  • M1 - Parallelization Optimization Techniques (Techniques d'optimisation de la parallélisation) (Modèles de performance, profilage, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • M1 - Numerical Computing (Calcul numérique) (Méthodes numériques)
  • M1 - Scientific Visualization (Visualisation scientifique) (Visualisation)
  • M1 - Parallel Machine Programming Project (Projet de programmation sur machine parallèle) (Passage de messages (MPI), Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Modèles de performance, profilage)
  • M2 - PAAM: Advanced programming of multicore architectures (Programmation avancée des architectures multicoeurs) (Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Architecture des ordinateurs)
  • M2 - APM: Architecture and Programming of Hardware Accelerators (Architecture et Programmation d'accélérateurs Matériels) (Programmation GPU, Architecture des ordinateurs)
  • M2 - EDP: Performance Evaluation (Evaluation De Performances) (Modèles de performance, profilage)
  • M2 - MPNA: Advanced Methods and Numerical Programming (Méthodes et Programmation Numériques Avancées) (Méthodes numériques)

Mémoire ou stage. S4 internship of 5-6 months in a research laboratory or industrial R&D (30 ECTS on UVSQ page; 24 ECTS in the Paris-Saclay catalogue)

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. Ter@tec technology park: collaborations with ARM, AWS, CEA, CGG, Eviden, Intel, NVIDIA, Paratools, SiPEARL (Paris-Saclay page). HPC hackathons (Teratec) where CHPS students take part. Research partners: LI-PARAD, Maison de la Simulation, ECR (Exascale Computing Research: Intel, CEA, GENCI, UVSQ), CMLA. Joint network seminars and shared internship offers across the CHPS masters network.

Limites de la fiche. Course topics are assigned from unit titles and the programme overview: the per-unit descriptions on the Paris-Saclay site load via JavaScript ('Charger le détail') and were not read, so topics not named in any title or overview are null, not 0. The MSCHP track course list on the Paris-Saclay page is JS-only; its units come from chps.uvsq.fr. No page read names a specific machine or cluster that students use, so machines_access is empty (unknown, not none). Internship ECTS differ between sources (30 on UVSQ, 24 in Paris-Saclay catalogue).

Calcul Intensif et Sciences des Données (CISD) - semester 9 of the ENSEIRB-MATMECA computer-science engineering degree, shared with the Université de Bordeaux M2 informatique

Confiance élevéeHPC (cœur)FR

Établissement ENSEIRB-MATMECA (Bordeaux INP) and Université de Bordeaux (joint track in the computer-science master)
Diplôme Diplôme d'ingénieur (Bac+5, 180 ECTS) specialisation / M2 informatique parcours CISD
Durée Engineering degree: 3 years after a Bac+2 entry (180 ECTS); the CISD specialisation is the 3rd-year semester 9 (30 ECTS) followed by a final-year semester 10 (content not read). Onisep lists the Université de Bordeaux computer-science master as 2 years, CISD being an M2 track.
Crédits Semester 9 CISD: 30 ECTS (sheet updated 12 Sept 2025). Whole engineering degree: 180 ECTS. Elements are weighted by coefficient (e.g. coef. 2,5), ECTS per element not given on the sheet.
Langue French (LV1 English unit; programme site written in French)
Modalité on-site
Admission Engineering degree entry level Bac+2 (admission via the ENSEIRB-MATMECA admissions); CISD is open to students of the ENSEIRB-MATMECA computer-science stream and to students of the Université de Bordeaux computer-science master. No separate M2 admission rules read.

Structure. Joint ENSEIRB-MATMECA and Université de Bordeaux track combining HPC and data science. Five teaching units: architectures, languages and programming models (MPI, OpenMP, GPU, HPC tools on the training cluster); HPC and learning algorithmics (parallel algorithmics and scheduling, data analysis, dense numerical linear algebra); runtime, extraction and visualisation (scientific visualisation, big-data processing, efficient deep learning, a 3D programming or quantum option); application modules (HPC application project, in-situ processing, data mining); languages and engineering culture. Engineering degree includes three internships totalling 12 months.

Cours du tronc commun (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

  • Langages du parallélisme (MPI, OpenMP, hybrid MPI+X) (Passage de messages (MPI), Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Algorithmique parallèle)
  • Multicoeurs et accélérateurs de calcul (CUDA, OpenCL, OpenMP/OpenACC directives, cuBLAS, Thrust) (Programmation GPU, Architecture des ordinateurs, Modèles de performance, profilage)
  • Outils et environnements pour le HPC (training cluster, software modules, job submission, performance analysis tools) (Ordonnancement de jobs, Modèles de performance, profilage)
  • Algorithmique du parallélisme, ordonnancement, structures de données, équilibrage de charges (Algorithmique parallèle, Ordonnancement de jobs, Efficacité énergétique)
  • Algorithmique numérique (dense linear algebra: Cholesky, LU, QR, SVD) (Méthodes numériques, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • Analyse de données (statistical learning, PCA, clustering, regression, classification, R) (Apprentissage automatique et profond)
  • Introduction à la visualisation scientifique (VTK, ParaView) (Visualisation)
  • Supports pour le traitement de données (Hadoop, HDFS, MapReduce, Spark, NoSQL) (Données massives, Systèmes distribués, Stockage et E/S parallèles)
  • Apprentissage profond efficace (model compression, activation recomputation and offloading, forms of parallelism, profiling) (Systèmes pour l'IA (entraînement distribué), Apprentissage automatique et profond, Modèles de performance, profilage)
  • Projet Application HPC (vectorisation, multithreading, distributed memory on linear-algebra kernels) (Modèles de performance, profilage, Passage de messages (MPI), Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Méthodes numériques)
  • Traitement des données in situ (spectral-element acoustic wave simulation, compression, visualisation formats) (Stockage et E/S parallèles, Simulation et applications, Visualisation)
  • Fouille, extraction et visualisation (Open Data University projects) (Données massives, Visualisation)

Mémoire ou stage. Projet Application HPC in semester 9; engineering degree has three internships totalling 12 months (final-year internship content not read)

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. Sectors listed by the programme: Airbus, Dassault, IBM, Thales, Total; CEA, EDF, IFP-EN. Industrial testimonials on the programme site from CEA DAM, ANEO and DDN Storage staff. Open Data University project subjects in the 'Fouille, extraction et visualisation' module. Shared HPC masters network seminars.

Limites de la fiche. Rare case with real unit descriptions (programme site gforgeron.gitlab.io/cisd), so 0 means the descriptions do not mention the theme. Scheduling is 1 because the course is about scheduling theory and the practical unit covers job submission on the training cluster; Slurm is not named on any page read. The training cluster is not named on the pages read, so machines_access is empty; PLAFRIM is named only in 'Projet Calcul Haute Performance', a course sheet of the sister MATMECA engineering stream (https://formation.bordeaux-inp.fr/plugins/odf-web/odf/_content/course-projet-calcul-haute-performance-fr-fr/Projet%20Calcul%20Haute%20Performance.pdf), not of CISD. The 2024 sheet listed Apprentissage profond efficace and Programmation 3D as a choice; the 2025 sheet and the site list the choice between Programmation 3D and quantum algorithmics. ECTS per element and the semester 10 content were not read.

Ingénieur ENSEEIHT Informatique et Télécommunications (Sciences du Numérique), parcours HPC et Big Data (S7 to S9)

Confiance élevéeMixteFR

Établissement Toulouse INP - ENSEEIHT
Diplôme Diplôme d'ingénieur (Bac+5, Master-level), 'parcours' within the FISE degree
Durée 3 years (engineering cycle, FISE full-time student status); HPC et Big Data parcours runs over semesters 7, 8 and 9 and is followed by a final-year project in semester 10. An apprenticeship (apprentis) version of the degree also lists this parcours.
Crédits 180 ECTS for the engineering degree; 30 ECTS per semester; S9 HPC et Big Data = 5 UE of 5 ECTS (25 ECTS) plus a SHS UE; final-year project (PFE) 30 ECTS
Langue French, with several units named and taught in English (e.g. Advanced Statistical Machine Learning, High Performance Scientific Computing, Inverse Problems)
Modalité on-site
Admission Not read on the pages fetched (the catalogue lists the 3-year engineering cycle; admission route not stated in the extracted text).

Structure. Engineering degree in computer science and telecoms with a 'HPC et Big Data' parcours. S7 and S8 give the foundations (functional programming, concurrent systems and middleware, optimisation and operations research, OpenMP, advanced linear algebra with Krylov and sparse methods, PDE, numerical simulation project). S9 has five UE: distributed systems and security (including a Calcul Parallèle unit with MPI and an introduction to GPU programming), advanced statistical machine learning, High Performance Scientific Computing (parallel numerical algorithms and performance analysis), inverse problems, and an elective UE (cloud and big-data infrastructure, cloud strategy, quantum mechanics for computing, etc.). S10 is the final-year project.

Cours du tronc commun (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

  • S9 - Systèmes et algorithmes répartis (consensus, checkpointing, distributed file systems) (Systèmes distribués, Résilience, checkpointing, Stockage et E/S parallèles)
  • S9 - Calcul Parallèle (supercomputer architecture, MPI, introduction to GPU programming) (Passage de messages (MPI), Architecture des ordinateurs, Réseaux et interconnexions, Programmation GPU)
  • S9 - Algorithmes pour le calcul à Hautes Performances (roofline, scalability, shared and distributed memory parallel algorithms) (Modèles de performance, profilage, Algorithmique parallèle, Architecture des ordinateurs, Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Passage de messages (MPI))
  • S9 - Méthodes itératives en algèbre linéaire (parallel solvers for PDE-derived and sparse systems) (Méthodes numériques, Simulation et applications, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • S9 - Advanced Statistical Machine Learning (statistical learning, deep learning on large data) (Apprentissage automatique et profond)
  • S9 - Inverse Problems (data assimilation, stochastic filtering, Bayesian analysis) (Méthodes numériques, Simulation et applications)
  • S8 - Open MP (in UE Applications concurrentes et communicantes, base de données) (Mémoire partagée (OpenMP))
  • S8 - Introduction aux méthodes de Krylov et à l'algèbre linéaire creuse / Projet Simulation Numérique (UE Algèbre linéaire avancée) (Méthodes numériques, Simulation et applications)
  • S7 - Systèmes concurrents / Intergiciels (UE Systèmes concurrents et communicants) (Systèmes distribués)
  • Soft and Human Skills (communication and English, careers and management, entrepreneurship, citizenship) (Gestion de projet, éthique)

Mémoire ou stage. Projet de fin d'études (PFE), 30 ECTS in semester 10, plus a 2A internship (6 ECTS)

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. S9 elective 'Projet USRP par SILICOM' (industrial partner project). Optimisation globale taught by an ENAC professor. PFE and 2A internship in companies (details not read). Member of the national Réseau des Masters CHPS (master-chps.fr): joint seminars, shared internships, Teratec hackathon.

Limites de la fiche. Toulouse has no standalone HPC master found; the HPC-oriented offer is the HPC et Big Data parcours of the ENSEEIHT engineering degree (Master-level). The search also showed a Mastère Spécialisé 'Ingénierie pour la Valorisation des Données Massives' (Bac+6, with a unit 'Calcul Haute performance et applications'), not read in detail. S9 descriptions of the mandatory UEs were read in full, so 0 for viz means they do not mention it; the S7/S8 sheets list titles only and the pages for the two 'Infrastructure for ...' electives are empty, hence null for sysadmin, scheduling, containers, ML systems and observability. Elective-based topics (cloud, bigdata, quantum) are scored 2 because a dedicated course names the theme, but they are optional. Page text is flagged 'indicatives et n'ont pas de valeur contractuelle'.

Master of Science in Informatics at Grenoble (MoSIG), Master Informatique UGA

Confiance moyenneCloud et distribuéFR

Établissement Université Grenoble Alpes (UFR IM2AG) and Grenoble INP - Ensimag
Diplôme MSc / Master Informatique (M1 + M2, Bac+5)
Durée 2 years (M1 + M2); students can apply to the full programme or to the 2nd year; M2 S10 is a 4 to 6 month internship/research project
Crédits 120 ECTS for the master; M2 semester 9 = 30 ECTS (24 ECTS in the chosen theme + 6 ECTS from another theme); S10 research project 30 ECTS
Langue English (the MoSIG track is taught entirely in English)
Modalité on-site
Admission M1: licence-level degree (BSc in computer science/engineering or equivalent, Bac+3 entry) on file and/or interview. M2: validated compatible M1 on file and/or interview. Open to Ensimag, Polytech Grenoble and UGA master students.

Structure. Research-oriented computer-science MSc with a broad first year (compilers, operating systems, networks, distributed systems, optional Parallel Algorithms and Programming with OpenMP/MPI labs) and a second year of thematic courses. The 2026-2027 M2 list includes Cloud Computing from infrastructure to applications (6 ECTS), Large scale Data Management (6), Distributed System (3), Virtualization (3), Advanced networking (6) and Scientific Methodology and Performance Evaluation (3), then a 30-ECTS research project. HPC proper (parallel algorithms, OpenMP, MPI) appears as one optional 3-ECTS M1 unit; the older 'Parallel systems' M2 unit seen in a 2021 sheet is not in the 2026-2027 list read.

Cours du tronc commun (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

  • M1 - Principles of operating systems (Architecture des ordinateurs)
  • M2 - Cloud Computing, from infrastructure to applications (virtualization: hypervisors, kernels, containers; microservices; cloud services) (Cloud et multi-fournisseurs, Conteneurs, Kubernetes, Systèmes distribués, Résilience, checkpointing)
  • M2 - Large scale Data Management (description not available) (Données massives)
  • M2 - Distributed System (Systèmes distribués)
  • M2 - Virtualization (Conteneurs, Kubernetes, Cloud et multi-fournisseurs)
  • M2 - Scientific Methodology and Performance Evaluation (Modèles de performance, profilage)
  • M2 - Research project (Mémoire, stage)

Mémoire ou stage. M1 compulsory 4-6 month internship or research project (second semester); M2 S10 research project of 30 ECTS, in a research lab or company R&D

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. Research teams listed for the Parallel, Distributed, Embedded Systems theme: Inria CORSE, LIG DRAKKAR, ERODS, MESCAL, MOAIS. Stage or research project can be done in a company R&D department. Labelled 'Core AI' by MIAI (Multidisciplinary Institute in Artificial intelligence); double degree possible with Swansea.

Limites de la fiche. HPC content is thin: one optional 3-ECTS M1 unit (Parallel Algorithms and Programming) and no M2 unit named HPC or GPU in the 2026-2027 list read; the 2021 sheet of the same master listed 'Parallel systems' (6 ECTS), 'Software infrastructure for data centers and Cloud computing' and 'Scheduling', now absent. Descriptions were read for only 2 of the units (Parallel Algorithms and Programming, Cloud Computing), so topic values rest on titles for the rest; 0 means no unit title in the whole M1/M2 list names the theme (numerics, simulation, quantum). The catalogue URL says 'catalogue-2021' but shows 2026-2027 and 2027-2028 programme tabs and is flagged as under construction pending a vote. mosig.imag.fr (official MoSIG site) was unreachable. The older CHPS-type Grenoble offer (Ensimag filière) was not found.

M2 Distributed and Parallel Computing (DiPaC), following the M1 DiPaQ

Confiance moyenneHPC (cœur)FR

Établissement Université Paris-Saclay (Master Informatique)
Diplôme M2
Durée M2 year: indicative dates 04/09/2026 to 30/09/2027; two teaching semesters (Semester 1 and Semester 2 in the unit table) plus a compulsory 6-month internship
Crédits Not stated as a total on the page read; the listed core units are 3 ECTS each (e.g. GPU programming 3 ECTS).
Langue English (all courses taught in English; French interactions possible)
Modalité on-site
Admission Strong computer-science background with parallel programming and distributed-systems basics, strong mathematics (linear algebra) and programming; natural continuation of the M1 DiPaQ; excellent related-master or engineering-school (double cursus) students admitted. 20 places; applications 15/04/2026 to 30/05/2026 on the Inception platform.

Structure. Advanced HPC and large-scale distributed-algorithms master taught in English. Core units cover GPU programming, data-parallel C++ programming, mobile-agent algorithms, nature-inspired algorithms, tensor computing and scientific computing, with a compulsory large-scale data management unit, one optional opening unit (ZX calculus, machine learning, signal processing or graph analytics), a soft-skill unit and a 6-month internship. Students specialise either in high-performance data analytics and AI (HPDA) or in hybrid HPC/quantum computing (HQI).

Cours du tronc commun (thèmes de la taxonomie entre parenthèses) :

  • Programmation GPU (GPU programming) (Programmation GPU)
  • Programmation à base du parallélisme de données en C++ (data-parallel programming in C++) (Mémoire partagée (OpenMP), Algorithmique parallèle)
  • Algorithmes d'agents mobiles (mobile-agent algorithms) (Systèmes distribués, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • Algorithmes de la nature (nature-inspired algorithms) (Systèmes distribués, Algorithmique parallèle)
  • Calcul tensoriel (tensor computing) (Méthodes numériques, Apprentissage automatique et profond)
  • Calcul scientifique (scientific computing) (Méthodes numériques, Simulation et applications)
  • [DKAI] Large-Scale Data Management and Data Quality (compulsory opening unit) (Données massives)

Mémoire ou stage. Compulsory 6-month internship on M2 DiPaC themes

Liens avec l'industrie et la recherche. Programme integrated into the Paris-Saclay ecosystem of research laboratories and industrial partners (partners not named on the page read). Double-cursus route with CentraleSupélec, Polytech Paris-Saclay and ENSTA Paris engineering students. Career targets: R&D engineer, ML engineer, research engineer, PhD.

Limites de la fiche. Per-unit descriptions load via JavaScript ('Charger le détail') and were not read; the page says seven fundamental HPC/parallel/distributed courses, but only six core units appeared in the extracted table, so one core unit may be missing from this record (possibly the one covering MPI/performance engineering: mpi is therefore null, not 0). Topic values 1 come from the competence and objective lists of the page (scheduling and load balancing on clusters and clouds, HPC software engineering, I/O and networks optimisation, responsible computing, fault tolerance), not from unit titles. The M2 total ECTS is not stated on the page read. Dates and admission data are for the 2026-2027 cycle.

Master of Computer Science, 'Computer Science: Concepts and Applications' (M1 + M2 Informatique fondamentale)

Durée M1 + M2 ; M2 : un semestre de cours (septembre-janvier) puis stage de recherche (fin janvier - mi-juin)
Crédits M2 : 60 ECTS (stage 30 ECTS + au moins 4 cours de 5 ECTS) ; M1 : unités de 5 ECTS + stage de 12 semaines = 12 ECTS (total M1 non lu)

Autour des diplômes : centres, écoles, compétitions

  • IDRIS (CNRS) - Jean Zay supercomputer, GENCI national resource (centre) : Real national GPU supercomputer (CPU, V100, A100 and H100 partitions) operated with Slurm, a Lustre parallel file system and InfiniBand/Omni-Path networks: the closest public, documented example of the cluster stack a compute infrastructure engineer runs. The hardware, scheduler and filesystem pages are public documentation to read and reproduce in labs.
  • IDRIS training catalogue (HPC and AI courses: MPI, OpenMP, GPU directives, Kokkos, Deep Learning Optimisé sur Jean Zay) (formation-courte) : Short, free (for CNRS and university staff) or CNRS-Formation-Entreprise courses taught by the Jean Zay support team, including a multi-GPU deep-learning scaling course; the course pages are public syllabi that fit a self-study path in parallel programming and distributed training. Registration site was under maintenance on the page read, so check dates.
  • Réseau des Masters CHPS (Calcul Haute Performance, Simulation) (réseau) : National network of French state-recognised HPC and simulation degrees (Paris-Saclay, Bordeaux CISD, Toulouse ENSEEIHT, Perpignan, Reims) with joint seminars, shared internship offers and a student hackathon with Teratec; the entry point to compare French HPC curricula.
  • CC-FR (centre de compétence national EuroCC France) (réseau) : Point d'entrée français du programme EuroCC : formations de prise en main d'un supercalculateur et formations avancées (containers, LLM) sur le réseau des mésocentres MesoNET, utile pour pratiquer sur de vraies machines hors université.
  • MesoNET (fédération des mésocentres français) (réseau) : Réseau de centres de calcul régionaux donnant accès à des supercalculateurs, du stockage et des heures de calcul : base du paysage HPC académique français en dessous du niveau national (GENCI).
  • ROMEO (Reims) : formations courtes HPC et multi-GPU (formation-courte) : Un centre de calcul académique qui propose des formations courtes sur GPU, multi-GPU et IA sur supercalculateur, ouvertes au-delà des étudiants du master CHPS.
  • Hackathon TERATEC (compétition) : Compétition HPC française à laquelle participent des étudiants (une équipe de M2 CHPS Reims y a obtenu le prix du jury en janvier 2024) ; bon terrain pour pratiquer l'optimisation sur machine réelle.
  • Cemosis (plateforme de modélisation et simulation, Université de Strasbourg) (centre) : Plateforme adossée au master CSMI, impliquée dans PEPR NumPEx (Exa-MA, SAGE-HPC) et le CoE EuroHPC HiDALGO2 : lien concret entre formation, HPC et projets exascale.
